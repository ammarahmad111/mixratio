Mixing tips

  1. Add the water first, then the product. It keeps soaps from foaming up.
  2. Measure product with a marked bottle or syringe, not by eye.
  3. Label every bottle with the product name and ratio.
  4. Stronger isn’t better. Over-concentrated mixes can stain or dry out surfaces.
  5. Test on a small hidden spot first.
